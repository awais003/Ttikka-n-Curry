—Our kitchen

Rooted in Peshawari tandoor, built for Islamabad evenings

Tikka n Curry started as a single tandoor and a promise: coals lit before opening, marinades made that morning, nothing reheated to order. Today the kitchen still runs the same way — charcoal-grilled tikka, karahi tossed to order in small batches, and biryani that goes from dum straight to your plate.

We seat groups comfortably for family dinners, keep a drive-through lane moving for a quick pickup, and run a seasonal iftar buffet that regulars plan their evenings around.
